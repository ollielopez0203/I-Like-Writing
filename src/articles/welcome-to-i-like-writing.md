This is a site for articles, op-eds, and whatever else I've made that doesn't have a better home. No comments section that fills up with noise, no algorithm deciding what you see first — just the writing, in order, with the option to filter by whatever you're in the mood for.

{% media "video", "https://www.youtube.com/embed/dQw4w9WgXcQ", "A video that felt relevant while I built this" %}

Every piece here can carry its own supporting material — a photo, a page from a book, a song, a clip — placed right where it matters in the text, rather than crammed into a generic gallery at the top. That was the whole point of building this instead of using a template.
