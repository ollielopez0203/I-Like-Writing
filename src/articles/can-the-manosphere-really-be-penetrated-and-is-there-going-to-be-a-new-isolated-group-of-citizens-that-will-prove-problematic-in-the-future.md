This whole manosphere thing is concerning me, and there are some implications that I can't get out of my head about it. Perhaps one of the most compelling forces driving my concern is having met and having been friends with a few of these people who have been ensnared. I think that the relationships I share with these people have shown me much, although I would say this is not unique as an experience. Indeed, what is most scary is that seemingly everyone now knows at least one person with the corrupted view of what masculinity should be.

Where to start? There is a lot to unpack about this theme. The type of people it inspires, the unfalsifiable logic behind it, and what it will mean for us in the coming years: I think these three specifics are the most useful to unpack. 

On the type of people that the manosphere has and does beguile, ensnare, captivate, delude, deceive, whatever you want to call it, there is no one specific mould. But there are a few consistencies. First, they are all men. Not young men specifically, although those are the more prominent demographic, for the ideas also appeal to older generations. 





WHat needs to be done --- reverse the stigma of empathetic masculinity = weak, fat, feminine men. We need fighters, bodybuilders, ceos, etc.
