**Im angry at the world in ways I can't articulate. And so I play music.**

This is not to say that this is the cause to my discontentment or the solution, but I am seemingly trying to understand why I have thoughts, or who I really am. Everything is a distraction from this and I cant shift my focus, buy into something 100%. Like my true kind of purpose, but even that alters the meaning. It feels like existentialism. I enjoy making observations about the connections to all things and this to me is learning about the world and life. I also find myself becoming and staying aware of the futility in actually searching for answers lately, and that brings out the humour in it for me; the great comedy of life. It also shows me that this will be the answer to the great question, and it's not even an answer. It's like searching your whole life for what is 1+1, and it's a fucking frog. Or 2 frogs? 

Like many people, I love to know why. I am curious, and it pushes me forward in this life. Why does it not depress me? Perhaps I feel a little detached from some of the core realities, perspectives and lifestyles that line up with my current view of how I want to express myself and who I want to be. The material side of that is irrelevant, it's just, what brings me closer to me? What brings me closer to my identity, to oneness? Of course, that silent desperation of crawling in the darkness to find that is the great comedy. I try so hard, I am desperately searching for something I don't know how to describe or conceptualise. And so, it is a great confusion. In moments of rare enlightenment; a content state of mind that is detached and thus free from the emotions grounded in my ego, I get a peek into what it all is. This big hurry, confusion and conflict that exists in my head, is a part of why we don't understand eachother. Living in fervent beliefs and delusions that we think gets us closer to the 'it'. We masturbate with the satisfaction of having reached the conclusion, finding the single answer to all of our strife. This manifests through our universal expression of self, we tell stories, write songs, sell eachother different niche inventions that we believe will bring us closer to the thing, and ultimately that blind searching is only resolved with an understanding and acceptance of it. The recognition that it is there, and that every little thing that happens in every moment in space and time is predicated by the previous moment in a continuous and infinite cycle back to the beginning of all time. That is why what I write is just an observation that it is happening, and why my writing is consistently riddled with admittance and concessions. Even that statement is paradoxical! I really cannot say I know the causal relationship of just about anything in this life. Life is this dynamic and precarious balance of faith or not. This is easy to do in a stimulating environment, and Earth has been stimulating enough. Prey, predators, our hierarchy of needs and desires to keep us alive, keep us surviving, and flourishing so that we can reproduce. But what next? After this, we have reached the extent of our programming; the same programming that we recognise in all living things, the very base programming. It is the response to an incredibly informationally dense and complex universe. It is our united struggle. It is the foundation of how we could possibly empathise and with extra terristrial life. The fracture of Self and oneness that gives us the ego. I did not ask to be born. But I am here. And so, there is a part of me, and a part of all of us that is that united struggle, the big hurry and great confusion. We spend all our waking time and conscious moments in preparing for the next conscious moment, to ensure we are able to survive. This thing, takes over to ensure your safety, to ensure you are safe in every sense of the word. That is also a fascinating connection to observe; that trauma is purely subjective and emotional. There is no fundamental value attached to anything, anywhere, at any time, because there is no hierarchy, hierarchy only exists in the view of our ego, when we start to separate this from that. And so, now I find myself searching for the connections between all things, fueled by our universal lizard brain, the need for love, belonging, connection, contentment, enlightenment, peace. The reason we can't sit still. The reason it's not quiet. Recent times showed me the part of me that is completely uncertain, independent of some other part of me. Or rather, I was beginning to feel the conflict. That I am something that only I am experiencing in every passing moment, but I am not the thoughts that whizz past. It comes from a part of myself that is trying so hard. I can't say I understand the relationship or difference with the parts within myself, I can only observe them and believe that they exist. They all interact with eachother and psychedelics seems to bring me back to the initial condition of the human, maybe when I was a kid, and unburdened by learnt experiences and trauma. Just pure presence, and existence. But even this is tainted by the lizard brain, the need to ensure my basic survival at any point. That part of myself, feels hurt and confused. And so the search continues, manifesting in every possible way in life. That search drives me, it is what I feel I need to express and do. Every material thing is inconsequential; it is just that process alone that unfolds over time, and born in any variation of environment may shape how it unfolds, but its that same process that unfolds regardless. Its another fascinating connection, the archetypes and tropes we observe in life, these uncanny synchronicities of everything at every level of reality. That connection between it all.


I can only recognise and observe a connection between these things. It is knowledge that all arbitrary notions aside, these things are connected. This makes sense intuitively, since everything comes from the same thing, it is all one, transcending the need for us and them, me and you. It's just us, and its just all this. And being limited to this very particular perspective of reality, shaped by our senses and sense of time, it is also the understanding that the search, that big hurry, is futile, and that there is no future or past, only now. Everything, everywhere, all at once. The Monad. God. Everything already happened and will happen, it just all exists simultaneously, but even that language seems to suggest an element of linear time. I suppose everything does. The archetype of beginning and end, birth and death.

I suppose, Ive also had the thought of, am I depressed? I truly believe that, atleast with my understanding and definition of depression, Im not. I think others would disagree, and really, the label and even concept doesnt matter. That is the beauty of it, that is why it doesn't matter to me! It is uncertain, there is no hierarchy or fundamental value to anything. There is stuff happening all of the time, everywhere. Maybe the meaning of everything is just entropy. Just the flow of energy, that pervasive life force that is searching for it. Metaphorical tendrils of entropy just trying to dissipate energy in various fantastic forms. And knowing that I know nothing, I guess I'm still alive because of the excitement of the unknown. Nothing is owed. Any kind of value is arbitrary, a result of thought, conceptualisation and language, that gives us these pre-ordained pockets of information and knowledge. Perhaps a fundamental flaw in our design is of our limits. I've not got the space to consistently absorb dynamic information about reality. But in reality, the universe is fluid and dynamic. In this plane of existence it's also bound by time, atleast in how we perceive it, and so energy flows, entropy occurs and time creeps forward. But our brains are lazy, it doesn't seem to have the capacity to absorb and understand the sheer vastness, complexity and infinte richness of it all. Love, creation and God. It may not be a biological limit. And so, my brain makes shortcuts. I mostly think with language, I categorise, recognise patterns, create arbitrary value and hierarchies and I will always find the relationship with anything, **because it is all just one thing**. And so, reality really is just a sandbox. But to survive the pace of life that we've demanded from ourselves, to think this freely means to not function according to arbitrary standards we have set for ourselves. These standards are powerful, and thus carry real world consequences. And so, completely arbitrary value and real world consequences are entangled. Challenging and disregarding these arbitrary societal standards impacts every facet of your life. Because we are helplessly reliant on eachother to survive, we have developed this system. It is impossible to conceptualise it all in its purest form. We must simplify, break it all down. Categorise, create value, create hierarchy.
